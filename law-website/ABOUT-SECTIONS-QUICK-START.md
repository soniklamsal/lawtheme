# Genius Law Section - Quick Start Guide

## Overview
The Genius Law section displays information about your law firm on the homepage. It appears after the Featured Services section and includes your firm's story and years of experience.

## How to Edit the Genius Law Section

### Step 1: Access the Genius Law Section
1. Log in to your WordPress dashboard
2. Look for **"Genius Law"** in the left sidebar menu (appears after Comments)
3. Click on it to open the editor

### Step 2: Edit the Content

#### Section Content
- **Title**: Main heading (e.g., "About Genius Law")
- **Subtitle**: Tagline below the title (e.g., "Your trusted legal partner with over 25 years of excellence")
- **Content**: Main description text - use the editor to format your text
  - You can add multiple paragraphs
  - Use bold, italic, bullet points, etc.
- **Years of Excellence**: Number of years (e.g., 25) - displays as a green badge on the image

#### Section Image
- **Upload Image**: Click "Upload/Select Image" to choose a photo
  - Recommended size: 800x600px or larger
  - The years badge will appear on the top-right of this image
- **Remove Image**: Click to remove the current image

### Step 3: Save Changes
1. Click **"Save Genius Law Section"** at the bottom
2. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
3. Visit your homepage to see the changes

## Where to Find Call-to-Action Buttons

The call-to-action buttons (like "Call Us Now" and "Email Us") are located on the **About Us page** template, not in this section. This keeps the homepage clean and focused on content, while the About page has the full contact options.

To edit the About page:
1. Go to **Pages** → **About** (or create one if it doesn't exist)
2. Select **Template: About Page** from the page attributes
3. The CTA section with buttons is built into the template

## Tips

1. **Keep Content Concise**: 2-3 paragraphs work best for readability

2. **Professional Image**: Use a high-quality photo of your office, team, or legal work

3. **Years Badge**: The years number appears as an attractive green badge overlay on your image

4. **Rich Text Editor**: Use the editor toolbar to:
   - Make text bold or italic
   - Create bullet lists
   - Add links
   - Format paragraphs

5. **Clear Cache**: After saving, always clear your browser cache to see changes immediately

## Troubleshooting

**Section not showing on homepage?**
- Make sure you have a title entered (required field)
- Click "Save Genius Law Section"
- Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)
- Try viewing in an incognito/private window

**Image not displaying?**
- Make sure you clicked "Save Genius Law Section" after uploading
- Try uploading a JPG or PNG file
- Check that the image file size is under 5MB

**Changes not appearing?**
- Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
- Clear WordPress cache if you have a caching plugin
- Try viewing in an incognito/private window
- Make sure you clicked "Save Genius Law Section"
